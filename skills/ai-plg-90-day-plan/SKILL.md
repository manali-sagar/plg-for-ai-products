# AI PLG 30/60/90-Day Plan

Turn a team's situation into a sequenced 90-day plan: observe how AI trust forms, fix the single biggest barrier, then build the habit loop and PQL signal. Based on *Your First 30/60/90 Days Building PLG for an AI Product* by Manali Hanamsagar (Small Table Studio).

The core idea: AI PLG first builds fail when teams redesign onboarding, instrument analytics, run A/B tests, and build a self-serve funnel all at once. For AI products this compounds, because users need time with the AI to calibrate trust. **The first 90 days are a structured evidence-gathering operation: you're learning what to build, not building everything at once.** The sequence is the discipline.

The full roadmap is in [references/framework.md](references/framework.md). Read it before building the plan.

## Step 1: Gather context

Ask for what you don't already have, in one short message:

- The AI product, the target user, and the core AI task
- What's already built: self-serve signup, onboarding, analytics/instrumentation, pricing
- What they know today about the three unknowns: their **real AI aha moment** (not the demo one), their **minimum output quality threshold**, and **the friction that stops non-experts** from reaching it
- Team size and who owns growth
- Any data: activation, retention at day 14, override/acceptance rates, conversion without sales
- What they're tempted to do right now

Never invent product details or metrics; mark unknowns.

## Step 2: Place them in the sequence

Decide which phase they're actually in, with reasoning:
- **Phase 1 (Days 1–30): Observe AI trust formation.** They can't yet say, from behavioral evidence, where users first trust an AI output and what breaks that trust.
- **Phase 2 (Days 31–60): Build the minimum viable AI trust path.** They know the biggest barrier but haven't shipped and measured one focused fix.
- **Phase 3 (Days 61–90): Build the AI habit loop and define the PQL.** Users can reach trust; the question is whether they return on their own and who's ready to convert.

Most teams that "already started" belong in Phase 1. Say so directly if that's the case.

## Step 3: Build the plan

Tailor each phase to their product:
- **Phase 1:** watch 10–15 new-user sessions focused on AI interactions (acceptance, overrides, abandonment, hesitation); pull retention to find the earliest AI interaction correlated with day-14 activity; interview 5–8 users who converted without sales ("What was the first AI output you trusted enough to use in real work?"); map the path from first prompt to first used output; interview sales about what they always explain about the AI in the first 10 minutes.
- **Phase 2:** pick the **one** intervention that most blocks non-experts from a usable first output: solve the blank-prompt problem, communicate output confidence, create a fast correction path, or instrument AI-specific events. Ship it, then watch 10 more sessions.
- **Phase 3:** identify the habit workflow (the AI task users return to most in weeks 1–2), design for return, place the upgrade moment after the 2nd or 3rd completion of the aha workflow, and define the AI-specific PQL (acceptance rate + override decay + workflow repetition).

## Step 4: Call out what not to do

Check what they're tempted to do against the four traps: benchmarking against other AI products, A/B testing AI output presentation before Phase 1, building a prompt optimization system before the trust path exists, hiring a full-time growth person before the behavioral baseline exists. Name any they're about to fall into.

## Output format

Produce a concise plan:

1. **Where you are** (2–3 sentences): the phase they're actually in and why
2. **The three unknowns**: what's known, what's not, and how each phase resolves them
3. **Phase-by-phase plan**: for each phase, the goal, 3–5 specific actions tailored to their product, an owner, and the success definition
4. **Metrics to instrument**: time to first accepted output, override rate by session number, export/downstream-use events, the PQL definition
5. **What you are NOT doing in the next 90 days**, specific to their temptations
6. **This week's first three actions**
7. **What's unknown** and how to find out

Separate observed facts from hypotheses.
